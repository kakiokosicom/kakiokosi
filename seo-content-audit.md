## Recommendations (Priority Order)

### Critical
1. **Add author attribution consistently** — Display "書き起こし.com編集部" on all articles; add `author` to Article JSON-LD
2. **Improve meta descriptions** — Generate unique, compelling descriptions from article excerpts (not just title repeats)

### High
3. **Publish new content** — Even 1-2 transcripts/month would reset freshness signals
4. **Add H2 subheadings to article content** — Parse existing content for natural section breaks; dramatically improves scannability and SEO
5. **Increase internal linking** — Articles averaging 3 links for 8k+ words; target 15-20 per article

### Medium
6. **Expand category descriptions** — Add 100-200 words per category explaining scope and key topics
7. **Add "Key Takeaways" section** to articles — Improves AI citation readiness with quotable summaries
8. **Noindex tag pages with < 3 articles** — Reduces thin content signals
9. **Add editorial standards** to About page — Document accuracy, review process

### Low
10. **Add related articles component** — Show 3-4 related transcripts per article
11. **Improve image alt text** in legacy content — Many WP-migrated images have generic alt
12. **Add FAQ schema** on About page — Answer common questions about the service
13. **Create llms.txt** — Machine-readable AI usage policy
